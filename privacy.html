CCTimer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

CCTimer ("the app", "we") is a free utility that helps Clash of Clans players track their in‑game upgrade timers. This policy explains what data the app handles. By using CCTimer you agree to this policy.

1. Information you enter

The account names, player tags, and game data (JSON) you import are stored only on your device (local storage / app preferences). This information is never sent to us or to any server — we do not operate a backend and cannot see it. Deleting an account in the app, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app removes this data from your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

CCTimer displays banner ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device information, and app usage. This processing is governed by Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use our services

3. Notifications

If you enable alerts, CCTimer schedules local notifications on your device to remind you before timers finish. These are generated on your device and are not sent through us.

4. Links to other services

The app may contain a link to an external donation page (Ko‑fi). When you tap it, your browser opens that site, which has its own privacy policy. We do not receive any payment or personal information through the app.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share data with third parties except as needed for the advertising described in section 2, handled by Google.

6. Your choices

  • Ads: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Your data: Delete accounts in the app or uninstall to remove all locally stored data.

7. Children

CCTimer is a general‑audience utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
